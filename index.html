About Japanese Practice

A simple space to learn, recognize, and practice Japanese characters. Choose Hiragana or Katakana, pick the characters you want, and practice for as long as you like.

Writing Quiz: see a character and type its romaji, or see the romaji and type the character.
Recognition Practice: see the romaji and pick the right character from four choices.

Show Yōon adds the combined sounds, like きゃ and キャ. Hiragana and Katakana keep their own selections and settings while the app is open.

Once installed, the app works offline.
